## New

- Discover adds a developer map of where apps come from. You can submit an app, or claim and update one you maintain. The form opens in the browser. Developer picks appear on Discover.
- Hermes can keep separate profiles. Each profile has its own model, memory, skills and sessions. A profile can connect to a Hermes panel on this Mac or over Tailscale.
- When Hermes needs a decision, you can approve a tool, answer a question, or provide a secret. Those cards can stay open together.
- Panel history lists recent, scheduled and archived sessions. You can include every profile on this Mac.

## Improvements

- Turning Mac control on grants full control and does not ask before each write. You can still turn individual capabilities off in Hermes settings.
- The Hermes conversation stays on the latest message, and the reasoning section is labeled.
- The local Hermes runtime is checked before it runs.
- Each Hermes profile window stays on that profile and does not pick up another profile's credentials.
- Assistant scrollbars stay narrow, so a long reply no longer shifts the text.

## Fixes

- Icons in the developer form no longer cover the text you type.
