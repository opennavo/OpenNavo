## Improvements

- The conversation list and composer show when another Hermes chat is replying, or waiting for your answer. Queued messages send in the order you wrote them.

## Fixes

- Switching chats or profiles no longer stops a Hermes reply, and it no longer drops a message that was already waiting.
- Hermes diagnostics follow the connection and profile you have open. A Panel connection no longer shows this Mac's local Hermes.
- Stopping a reply marks it as stopped instead of as a finished answer. If a message with files cannot send yet, the text and files you chose stay in the composer.
- The Homebrew cask download includes the app version and requires macOS Ventura.
